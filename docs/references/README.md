## How to Use

### For Thesis Writing

1. **Open BIBLIOGRAPHY.md** for full citations in APA 7th format
2. **Copy citations** directly into your references section
3. **Use "Quick Reference by Topic"** section to find relevant papers for each section

### For Understanding Design Decisions

Each paper in BIBLIOGRAPHY.md includes:
- **Key Finding:** Main result relevant to our work
- **Relevance:** How it influenced our dataset design
- **DOI/URL:** Direct link to paper

### Topic-Based Navigation

**Need to justify theme distribution?**
→ See papers: Schredl & Göritz (2018), Schredl (2010), Zadra & Donderi (2000)

**Need to explain confidence estimation?**
→ See papers: Tam et al. (2024), Chen et al. (2024), Farquhar et al. (2024)

**Need IRT protocol citations?**
→ See papers: Krakow & Zadra (2006), Krakow et al. (2001)
