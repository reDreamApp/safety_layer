# **Multidimensional Mastery Scale (MMS) Scoring Manual**

**Source:** Germain, A., Krakow, B., Faucher, B., Zadra, A., Nielsen, T., Hollifield, M., ... & Koss, M. (2004). *Increased Mastery Elements Associated With Imagery Rehearsal Treatment for Nightmares in Sexual Assault Survivors With PTSD*. Dreaming, 14(4), 195-206.

## **1\. Overview**

The Multidimensional Mastery Scale (MMS) assesses the degree of control or mastery a dreamer exercises over disturbing dream elements. It was developed specifically to measure changes in dream content following Imagery Rehearsal Therapy (IRT).

The scale consists of **six subscales**.

## **2\. General Coding Rules**

* **Unit of Analysis:** Code specific **occurrences** of mastery within the dream report.  
* **Scoring:** Count the number of times each type of mastery occurs.  
* **Normalization:** To control for report length, the authors recommend dividing the total count of mastery occurrences by the total word count of the dream report (excluding redundancies and external references).

## **3\. The Six Mastery Subscales**

### **I. Behavioral Mastery**

**Definition:** The dreamer actively performs an action to change the dream course to their advantage.

**Scoring Criteria:** Score **Behavioral Mastery** if the dreamer:

* Performs an action to alter the course of the dream to the advantage of the dreamer.  
* Fights back against a threat.  
* Wins over a threat or aggressor.  
* Makes behavioral attempts to obtain assistance.

### **II. Social Mastery**

**Definition:** Changes related to other characters, social interactions, or receiving help.

**Scoring Criteria:** Score **Social Mastery** if the dreamer:

* Changes the personality aspects of other dream characters.  
* Removes threatening characters.  
* Changes the nature of an interaction with another character.  
* Adds a helpful new character.  
* Adds a non-threatening social setting.  
* Is assisted by another character **on the dreamer's request**.

### **III. Environmental Mastery**

**Definition:** Changes to the physical setting or spontaneous helpful events.

**Scoring Criteria:** Score **Environmental Mastery** if the dreamer:

* Changes the physical environment to a non-threatening setting.  
* Makes the initial dream environment impermeable to threat.  
* Experiences a **spontaneous occurrence of a non-requested helpful event**.

### **IV. Emotional Mastery**

**Definition:** Changes in the dreamer's emotional state or reaction.

**Scoring Criteria:** Score **Emotional Mastery** if the dreamer:

* Changes the overall dream affect (emotional atmosphere).  
* Changes their emotional reactions to specific dream characters.  
* Changes their emotional reactions to specific dream events.  
* Changes their emotional reactions to specific dream settings.

### **V. Mythical Mastery**

**Definition:** Interventions involving supernatural or divine elements.

**Scoring Criteria:** Score **Mythical Mastery** if:

* A supernatural figure intervenes in favor of the dreamer to release them from the threat.  
* A supernatural event intervenes to terminate the threat.  
* A supernatural figure/event reassures the dreamer (even without changing the course of the dream).  
* The dreamer uses supernatural powers to overcome the threat.

### **VI. Avoidance**

**Definition:** Escaping the threat without resolving it (often considered a lack of mastery, but coded within this system).

**Scoring Criteria:** Score **Avoidance** if the dreamer:

* Awakens from the dream to escape perceived threats.  
* Avoids unpleasant dream scenes within the dream **without changing them** (e.g., closing eyes, looking away, "dying" to stop the horror).

## **4\. Coding Summary Table**

| Code | Type | Key Indicator |
| ----- | ----- | ----- |
| **BM** | **Behavioral** | Dreamer acts, fights, or asks for help. |
| **SM** | **Social** | Character changes, social setting changes, or receiving requested help. |
| **EM** | **Environmental** | Setting becomes safe/impermeable, or *spontaneous* help occurs. |
| **EmoM** | **Emotional** | Affect or emotional reaction changes. |
| **MM** | **Mythical** | Supernatural powers or divine intervention. |
| **AV** | **Avoidance** | Waking up or avoiding the scene without changing it. |
