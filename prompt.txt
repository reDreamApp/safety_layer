The literature did not provide a ready-made computational safety ontology for IRT.
The category set was therefore constructed for this thesis by combining two sources
of guidance. The adaptive categories follow the MMS mastery dimensions. The
maladaptive categories capture rewriting actions that would be problematic for an IRT-
style rescripting task: escaping the scene, ending the dream, repeating it unchanged,
escalating into destructive revenge, or denying the emotional impact of distressing
material. These categories are not presented as the only possible ontology, but as a
concrete and reviewable formalization that can be implemented, tested, and revised.
